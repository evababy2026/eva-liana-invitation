MUSIC BOX WALTZ - ONE-TIME SETUP

1. Download the free MP3 from https://pixabay.com/music/lullabies-music-box-waltz-163673/
2. Rename the downloaded MP3 to music-box-waltz.mp3
3. Upload music-box-waltz.mp3 alongside index.html, door-floral.png, and share-preview.jpg to your GitHub repository.
4. The music starts after the guest taps to open the floral doors, loops softly, and can be paused or resumed with the music button.

NOTE: The MP3 is NOT included in this ZIP because Pixabay's file download could not be retrieved here. The button cannot play music until you add it.
